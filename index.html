FixPoint: Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

FixPoint is a GPS survey app: it marks points, measures land, stamps photos and exports your data. It is built so that your data stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location is read from your phone's GPS while you use the app. It is used on the phone to show your position, mark points, measure areas, stamp photos and navigate. FixPoint does not send your location to us.
  • Points, areas, photos, notes and projects are stored only on your phone. FixPoint has no account and no cloud storage.
  • Backups and exports are files you create and choose where to send (for example to Google Drive, WhatsApp or email). They go only where you send them.

When FixPoint uses the internet

  • Maps: to show map and satellite images, the app downloads map tiles from the map providers you choose: OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap), EOX (Sentinel-2), Esri, OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap, or a map source you add yourself. Like any website, these servers see your internet address and which map area is requested. They do not receive your GPS position, your points or your photos.
  • Place search: when you search for a place name, the text you type is sent to Photon (by komoot), which suggests places as you type, and to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find matching places. To show nearby places first, both services also receive the area of the map you are looking at, which is often around where you are. Your points and photos are not sent.
  • Sharing: when you share your location by SMS, WhatsApp or another app, FixPoint prepares the message and your phone's own app sends it to the people you choose.

Testing versions only: problem reports

Testing versions (TestFlight and Google Play testing) include a "Report a problem" button. When you send a report, it includes what you choose to attach (a screenshot or screen recording) and your note. It also includes the app version, phone model, operating system version, recent app logs and the app's current screen and settings. It goes to the developer's own server, only to fix problems. Dictated notes are turned into text on your phone; audio is never sent. Testing versions may also send crash details when the app crashes. Store versions do not include this feature.

What FixPoint does not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking and no analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No account, so no email address or password is collected.

Permissions

  • Location: to mark points and measure land. Precise location is needed for useful accuracy.
  • Camera: to take stamped photos.
  • Photos: to save stamped photos and to stamp existing photos you choose.
  • Microphone and speech recognition (testing versions only): to dictate a problem report.

Deleting your data

Delete a point, area or project in the app, or uninstall FixPoint to remove everything it stored. Problem reports sent from testing versions can be deleted on request.
